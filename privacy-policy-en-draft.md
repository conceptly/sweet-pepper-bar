2.1. The Operator uses data for specific, lawful purposes established in advance, limits the data to what those purposes require, and does not combine information for incompatible purposes. Retention depends on the purpose and applicable legal requirements.

## 4. Forms and contact details

4.1. Forms identify information needed to handle the request as required. Without a usable contact, a reply may be impossible. You do not need to submit a form to browse the menu.

4.2. Where a form relies on consent, you separately select an initially unticked checkbox before sending it. The consent text is available beforehand, and the Operator retains evidence that consent was given. Reading the Policy does not replace separate consent.

> **Before publishing consent:** check that /consent/ and /en/consent/ work without a WordPress login, the text version is current, and a received message includes the consent record. The code checks the checkbox and records version, time and IP; the supplied test messages do not show that record.

4.3. The Operator uses your contact details to respond to the matter you raised. Sending a message does not subscribe you to advertising. Receiving a message through a messenger does not remove the Operator’s responsibilities for that information.

4.4. Website form messages are sent through Timeweb’s email service to hello@sweetpepper.bar. Yandex Mail automatically collects messages from that mailbox and stores them in the Operator’s director’s mailbox (p020885@yandex.ru). Copies of enquiries also arrive at the general manager’s work mailbox on Yandex Mail, used for replying to and handling guests’ questions. Deletion covers copies in both services and other working copies. Telephone calls are not recorded. The website theme does not create a separate archive of message bodies in the WordPress database; mailboxes and any technical copies held by providers are accounted for separately.

4.5. To limit repeated submissions, the server temporarily stores a hash of the IP address and a submission counter. The counter expires one hour after it is updated; WordPress removes expired technical records. The email includes the form data, selected team recipients, page language and URL, and a consent record containing the date, time, text version and IP address.

It does not establish citizenship or precise location, and this function does not separately retain its result; the IP may appear in logs and form-submission evidence.

When a map loads, Google receives technical information including the IP address and browser details, and may use its own cookies. “Got it” only dismisses the information notice; it does not constitute consent to maps or analytics.

> **Google remains unresolved:** describing automatic loading reflects the code, not a finding that this setup is lawful. A US/Canadian IP does not exempt the Operator from applicable requirements. The legal basis and cross-border review in section 7 remain open.

You can also clear cookies and website storage in your browser’s privacy settings and restrict third-party cookies. Clearing settings may cause language suggestions or notices to reappear. For a website, this can arise from foreign services, overseas storage or access by a foreign contractor. A Russian-language interface or .ru domain does not establish where processing occurs.

## 8. Retention and destruction

8.1. Data is kept only as long as needed for its purpose unless legislation or an applicable agreement provides otherwise. Section 3 sets the periods and conditions. Completing an enquiry does not authorise using its data for another purpose without a separate basis.

8.2. Correspondence history has the separate purpose and period in section 3; answering the initial enquiry does not itself end that separately agreed purpose. When the relevant purpose or period ends, or consent is withdrawn, data is generally destroyed within 30 days if no other lawful basis for processing and retention exists. Confirmed inaccuracies are corrected within seven working days. Requests to cease processing are handled within ten working days, subject to statutory exceptions and permitted, reasoned extensions.

8.3. Deletion covers working systems, both email services—Timeweb and Yandex—files and paper records, and processors acting on the Operator’s behalf. Electronic data is removed in a way that prevents further use; paper records are destroyed in a way that prevents reconstruction. Destruction is documented as required.

8.4. Backups support recovery and are protected from ordinary access. Data due for destruction is excluded from further operational use, including following a restoration. Backup deletion must meet legal deadlines; where timely destruction is objectively impossible, statutory restriction and subsequent destruction procedures apply. Backups do not justify indefinite retention.
