## Category previews and section eyebrows

### Breakfast

**Section eyebrow:** Whenever your morning starts

Some mornings start at noon. Breakfast is here all day, at the same price whenever yours begins.

### Lunch

**Section eyebrow:** Weekdays 12 to 4

Salad, soup, a hot dish and a drink — put together your weekday lunch. A break in the day, at your own pace.

### Bar Snacks

**Section eyebrow:** Share with friends

Boards, pickles, wings and something for the middle of the table. Pick a drink to go with them — the bar can help.

### Salads

**Section eyebrow:** Fresh & crisp

A proper Cobb, a Sicilian with oranges, or your favourite Caesar. Something fresh for the side — or a meal of its own.

### Sandwiches & Bagels

**Section eyebrow:** House-made sesame bagels

Bagels and sandwiches, generously filled. Pick your favourite, settle in and make a meal of it.

### Soups

**Section eyebrow:** Warm & comforting

Borscht with salo croutons, creamy pumpkin or mushroom soup. Something warm to start with — or the reason you came.

### Hot Dishes

**Section eyebrow:** From the kitchen

Pasta, steaks, dishes from the grill and Yaroslavl-style roast. A proper plate for a proper appetite.

### Desserts

**Section eyebrow:** Always room for something sweet

Apple strudel with ice cream, raspberry Napoleon or a slice of cheesecake. Save room — or take something sweet home.

### For Kids

**Section eyebrow:** Small plates, big favourites

Pancakes, syrniki, nuggets and pasta from the same kitchen, in smaller portions. Something familiar for the youngest at the table.

### House Infusions

**Section eyebrow:** Made in-house

From cranberry and salted caramel to raspberry gin. Familiar favourites, something unexpected, and a little heat if you fancy it.

### Cocktails

**Section eyebrow:** Shake & stir

Classics, house twists and something new to try. Pick a favourite, or ask the bar for a suggestion.

### Wine

**Section eyebrow:** By the glass or bottle

A glass with dinner or a bottle for the table. Red, white, sparkling — with sherry and vermouth keeping company.

### Beer

**Section eyebrow:** Cold & crisp

Bottled and on tap, cold enough to settle arguments. There's a bell on the counter that says “ring for a beer” — it works.

### Spirits

**Section eyebrow:** Neat or on the rocks

Whisky, tequila, gin and the rest of the back bar. Something familiar, or a new name for your next favourite.

### No Buzz

**Section eyebrow:** Zero proof, full flavour

Smoothies, alcohol-free cocktails, lemonades and milkshakes. Plenty of flavour, whatever your plans for the evening.

### Tea & Coffee

**Section eyebrow:** Brewed with love

Espresso, cappuccino and signature teas. Start the day, pause after lunch or stay for another cup.
