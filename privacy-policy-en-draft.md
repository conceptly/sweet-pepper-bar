## 4. Contact forms and email

4.1. Only the data necessary to handle the relevant request is mandatory in the website forms.

4.2. Where the legal basis for processing is consent, it is given separately from other documents and information that the user confirms. Before submitting a form, the user can read the text of the relevant consent.

4.3. Sending an enquiry does not mean consent to advertising mailings.

4.4. Messages from the website forms may be sent through the Timeweb email service used by the Operator to hello@sweetpepper.bar, and then automatically forwarded to the Yandex Mail mailbox used by the Operator. Copies of enquiries may be kept in the work mailboxes of persons who need access to reply to enquiries.

4.5. The Operator does not record telephone conversations.

4.6. To limit repeated submissions, the website may temporarily process a technical hash of the IP address and a submission counter. This information is used only for the technical protection of the form and is deleted once the necessary technical period has expired.

## 5. Cookies, local storage and external services

5.1. The website uses cookies and the browser's local storage for the technical operation of the website and to save user settings.

5.2. In particular, the website may use the following entries:

| **Entry** | **Purpose** | **Period** |
|---|---|---|
| `sp-lang` | Saves the selected language | Until the choice is changed or the website data is deleted in the browser |
| `spMapPermission` | Saves the choice regarding Google Maps | 180 days |
| `spAnalyticsPermission` | Saves the choice regarding Yandex Metrica | 180 days |
| `sp-nudge-off` | Remembers that the language-switch suggestion was closed | 30 days |
| `sp-landed` | Limits repeated display of the suggestion within a session | For the session |
| `spRailLearned` | Remembers interaction with the navigation | For the session |

5.3. The values of these local entries used for website settings are not sent by the Operator to the server as personal data in their own right.

5.4. Yandex Metrica runs only after the visitor's separate consent has been obtained. Until consent is obtained, the Metrica code does not run on the website.

5.5. When Yandex Metrica is used, the information processed includes, in particular, information about visits, the browser and device, IP address, pages viewed, referral source and the visitor's actions on the website. Webvisor may record the visitor's actions for later playback.

5.6. The contents of the contact form fields — name, telephone number, email address and message text — must not be passed to or recorded in Metrica. The Operator does not pass contact details from the forms to Metrica and does not use Metrica for advertising or to identify visitors on its own.

5.7. Yandex processes the personal data of website visitors on the Operator's instructions in accordance with the Yandex Metrica terms of use. Yandex expressly states that the user of the service is the personal data operator, and that Yandex is a person acting on the operator's instructions.

5.8. Webvisor recordings are kept by the service for no more than 15 days, including the current day.

5.9. The embedded Google map does not load automatically when the page is opened. Until the visitor makes a choice, a placeholder and a “Load map” button are shown in its place.

5.10. Pressing “Load map” is a separate choice by the visitor regarding loading Google Maps. When the map loads, the browser connects to Google's infrastructure, as a result of which Google may receive the IP address, information about the browser and device, and other technical data. Google's processing of this data is also governed by Google's documents.

5.11. The Operator does not use the visitor's precise GPS coordinates to show the venue address.

5.12. Previews of VK posts and their images may be loaded in advance to the website server. When a visitor follows a link to a post, the external VK service opens, and data processing there is governed by its own documents.

5.13. The choice regarding Yandex Metrica and Google Maps can be changed at any time through the “Privacy settings” link at the foot of the website. Turning off Metrica stops further data collection on the website. Turning off Google Maps stops subsequent automatic loading of the embedded map until permission is given again.

5.14. Deleting cookies and local storage in the browser does not mean deleting data already received by the Operator or an external service. To withdraw consent given earlier, use “Privacy settings” or contact the Operator.

## 7. Storage of personal data and cross-border transfer

7.1. When collecting personal data of citizens of the Russian Federation, the Operator ensures compliance with the requirements of Part 5 of Article 18 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ on the use of databases located in the Russian Federation.

7.2. The Operator must not use databases located outside the Russian Federation for the recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification and retrieval of personal data of citizens of the Russian Federation, except in cases expressly provided for by law.

7.3. Using Google Maps after the visitor's choice may involve the transfer of the browser's technical data to Google. The Operator treats such a transfer separately from the primary storage of personal data in Russian databases, and it is carried out in compliance with the applicable legal requirements on the cross-border transfer of personal data.

7.4. If a cross-border transfer of personal data arises for a service used, the Operator fulfils the requirements of Article 12 of Federal Law No. 152-FZ, including notifying the authorised body in the cases and manner established by law.

7.5. The Operator does not make statements about where an external service processes data solely on the basis of that service's domain name or interface language.

## 8. Retention periods and destruction

8.1. Personal data is kept no longer than the purposes of its processing require, unless another period is established by federal law, a contract or another lawful basis.

8.2. Once the purposes of processing have been achieved, or when another lawful ground for ending processing arises, personal data is to be destroyed or depersonalised, unless its further storage is required by law.

8.3. When consent is withdrawn, processing stops unless the Operator has another lawful basis for continuing it.

8.4. Data is destroyed by the Operator and, where applicable, by persons processing data on the Operator's instructions, in a way that rules out any further use of the data, taking into account the technical features of the relevant systems and backups.
