6.1. Consent is valid for 180 days from the choice on the site, or until withdrawn. After that the site asks again; Metrica does not start until a new choice is made.

6.4. After withdrawal or expiry the Operator stops processing and destroys the data obtained, including at the processor, within 30 days unless another legal basis applies [CHECK: how to delete one visitor's data in Metrica]. Cookies already stored can be deleted in the browser settings.

6.5. Refusing consent does not limit use of the site. More detail: [Personal Data Processing Policy](https://sweetpepper.bar/en/privacy-policy/), section 5.4.
