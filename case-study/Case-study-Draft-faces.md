# Sweet Pepper: Case Study Plan (The Five Faces)
*Target: Thematic / Modular Structure*

## The Hook (Results First)
*TL;DR, high-fidelity visuals of the day/night modes, and the core 50/50 problem: 1 venue, 4 daily identities, 0 generic templates.*

## Face 1: The Visual System
*Taming 12 years of chaotic artifacts.*
* The strict 3-hue generative color rule.
* The typography logic (Molot for display, Golos for reading).
* Auditing your own accessibility claims (fixing contrast ratios and the "colour-blind safe" oversight).

## Face 2: The Core UX Mechanic
*The "Heat = Time" concept.*
* How the hospitality "no-clock rule" birthed the spice slider.
* Why real usability testing forced a pivot from the slider control to the final 4-tile photo grid.

## Face 3: Voice & Content Architecture
*Words as the interface.*
* The dynamic status lines ("lunch till 4 — no rush").
* Building the menu matrix.
* Treating the Russian/English layers as "register twins" rather than 1:1 translations.

## Face 4: The Reality Build & Constraints
*Shipping real software in a constrained environment.*
* Dealing with Geopolitics: OFAC sanctions ruling out Webflow, forcing a self-hosted WordPress build.
* Managing Russian data laws and localizing the map/social experiences.
* Building a backend that the venue team can actually operate.

## Face 5: The Details & Polish
*The interaction vocabulary.*
* The page loader toy (the slider's final home).
* The "bartender's ticket" UI.
* Using the browser as the final drawing board and logging decisions in `testing.md`.

## Reflection
*Systems outliving the launch.*
* Systemizing beats redesigning.
* Loud color needs more rules, not fewer.
* Iterating the layout, not just the polish.
