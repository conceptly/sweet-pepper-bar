## Editorial approach

The food has enough character already. Keep the familiar dish descriptions, make the new ingredients easy to scan, and put the playful voice into a few headings and invitations. The fall file mostly needs reconciliation, not a wholesale rewrite.

Sources: `design.md` §1–1.2 (cosy/spicy, guest perspective, RU/EN as register twins); `russian-website-voice.md` → *Как звучит этот голос* and *Соцсети → сайт*; `website-brief.md` → *Menu page*, *Seasonal & specials* and *Menu storage*; `menu-en.md`, `menu.md`, `menu-copy-en.md` and `menu-copy-review-en.md`. The team's document supplies menu content; its promotional prose and service notes are not instructions to change the site.

The English file retains 64 tables and all 289 item rows in the team's order, with prices and stated portions unchanged. Existing catalogue descriptions are carried forward, including details omitted from the abbreviated fall file. Ingredient lists and choices are consolidated into their item rows. Explicit recipe conflicts are marked and both versions are recorded in its editorial appendix. The later website copy takes precedence for the infusions introduction and the three reviewed infusion descriptions. New dishes get only ingredients actually supplied by the team.

## Changes I would make to the website

1. **Update the menu records, not the navigation.** Keep Breakfast, Lunch, Bar Snacks, Salads, Sandwiches & Bagels, Soups, Hot Dishes, Desserts and Kids; keep the current bar categories and the shared No Buzz / Tea & Coffee sections. The English file follows the source order for comparison, not as a proposed website order. In particular, Beer remains its own category rather than a subsection of Cocktails, and Soups stays separate from Hot Dishes.
2. **Place autumn additions in their natural categories.** Dakk, the autumn sweet potato salad and caponata belong in Salads; the new cocktails in Cocktails; seasonal lemonades in No Buzz; barberry tea and melon-strawberry Raf in Tea & Coffee; the milkshake in No Buzz. The autumn soup of the day is explicitly a Lunch item—do not automatically add it to the full-price Soups list. Seasonal highlights should reference those same dish/drink records. This follows *Seasonal & specials*: “Seasonal is a tag, not a section.” The current implementation selects records in the menu page's seasonal tab; the brief's query wording is a design intention, not a claim about automatic selection today.
3. **Replace the summer highlight selection and any remaining summer heading.** EN: **Autumn menu highlights**. Keep the current RU **Сезонные новинки** if you want the heading to last across seasons. Suggested food candidates: the autumn sweet potato salad, caponata and peanut cake; bar candidates: Coquette, Red Moscow and Barbara Collins. These are editorial choices, not a claim that every new item is season-limited. Only use cards once a matching photo and complete item record exist. Retain the established section-link labels; no new “Discover” copy is needed.
4. **Remove old summer references from active placements after reconciliation.** Check the lists, highlights, photo captions, Home previews and both directions of the pairing picker, including About. Keep reusable records archived/hidden rather than deleting their history. Existing descriptions for continuing dishes stay; a new item at an old price does not inherit the old recipe or photo.
5. **Keep most category introductions.** Breakfast, salads, sandwiches, mains, desserts, cocktails and No Buzz already fit. The Bar Snacks paragraph mentions “boards, pickles”; pickles are not separately listed here. Ask whether Meat Set 2026 includes them. If not, use the short replacement below. Keep the existing lunch hours and all-day breakfast message; the fall source supports both.

## Resolve before publishing the affected rows

The English appendix contains the exact old/new recipe wording and row markers.

- **Recipes:** borscht gains mushrooms but drops the crouton reference; New York Sour changes whisky; Coco Mademoiselle changes foam; Mai Tai no longer lists orange juice. Confirm the changes rather than treating them as tone edits.
- **Missing recipes:** Alfredo and alla Norma need actual ingredients. “Alfredo” is not permission to invent cream, chicken or a particular cheese. The two new seasonal salads lack weights; lunch rows and shots also lack portions in this source. Do not fill these from assumptions.
- **Caponata:** feta appears in the title but not the ingredients. Confirm the tomato/balsamic/mustard sauce wording too.
- **Names:** Да-Манш vs Ла-Манш; Эйнгирл vs Эйприл; Ан-99 vs Аи-99; Сикстрет vs the existing Quickshot. Preserve continuity while checking the actual print artwork or team master. Dakk is a provisional transliteration of Дакк; it is not a duck salad.
- **Bottle identity:** do not transfer Lakky's tasting notes to Camden Park. Check the source's brand spellings and whisky categories against the bottles. “Tararasa” in the Sour conflicts with “Tarapaca” in the wine list. The review does not independently authenticate brands or origins.
- **Offers:** keep the documented same-flavour condition and Advocaat exclusion with 3 + 1 until explicitly changed. Keep the lunch offer to tea, coffee, juice, mors or cola with a hot dish; confirm which coffee/tea rows qualify before implying every speciality drink is half-price. The breakfast glass remains 125 ml / 260 ₽; the regular wine-list glass is 345 ₽, which is a separate price, not a conflict. Absence of older Wednesday/takeaway/selected-main offers from this file does not itself revoke them.
- **Dietary information:** the green-leaf legend survived, but individual leaf markers did not. Retain only documented existing labels pending kitchen review; don't guess new vegetarian or vegan badges. Keep walnut pesto, peanuts, egg white and similar ingredient details visible. The kids' latte should say “without coffee”, not make the broader old “caffeine-free” claim.

### The deliberately wrong option

**WE'VE GOT A SOFT SPOT FOR AUTUMN.** / **У НАС К ОСЕНИ СЛАБОСТЬ.**

This deliberately breaks `design.md` §1.1: the venue speaks as “we” outside a ticket/status reply. I think it could earn a one-season exception as a small introduction above the highlights: it sounds like a person looking forward to feeding you, and the sweet potato/peanut/foam selection gives the fondness something concrete to point to. It would not earn replacing the clear category names or dish descriptions. My default is still the guest-facing version above; no rule or published copy has been changed.
