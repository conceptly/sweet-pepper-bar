## 3. Actions with personal data

This consent covers the collection, recording, systematisation, accumulation, storage, clarification (updating, modification), retrieval, use, transfer (provision of access) in the cases set out in this document, depersonalisation, blocking, deletion and destruction of personal data.

## 4. Recipients and processors

To receive and handle enquiries, personal data may be processed by ООО «Перец» and by persons it engages under contracts and personal data processing instructions, in particular the email and technical services the operator uses. Such persons process the data only within the relevant purposes and instructions.

## 5. Duration and withdrawal

This consent is valid until the purpose of processing is achieved or until it is withdrawn, unless Russian law or a contract provides another basis for continued processing. Withdrawal can be sent to the operator in any form by email to p020885@yandex.ru or to the operator's postal address.

## 6. Important limitations

This consent is not consent to advertising mailings and does not cover processing for direct marketing. Such purposes require separate prior consent.

The contents of the website's form fields are not passed to Yandex Metrica and are not recorded by Webvisor. Processing in Yandex Metrica is based on a separate consent published on the website.
