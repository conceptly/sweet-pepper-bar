---

## What belongs here

Connector wording for **Home, About, Visit and Menu** (both menu states), EN and RU. Nothing else — section names live in `website-brief.md` → Section labels, hero copy in `menu-hero-copy.md`.

Connectors are non-interactive display text. They are not headings, not links, not photo captions. Treat the wording as **display copy**: it carries tone, not information, and no fact should be reachable only through a connector.

---

## Rules the copy has to satisfy

1. **Short bursts.** Molot at display scale, 3–7 words (`design.md` §3.2). Longer and the band stops reading as texture.
2. **Register.** Section names are plain and each list gets exactly one voiced name; connectors are a separate register and aren't bound by that count — but they are the page's whole wink budget. Judge them **as a set, per page**, not one at a time.
3. **Guest perspective** (`design.md` §1.1). The guest owns "your"; the venue speaks as "we" only in ticket replies and status bands — so a connector never says "we".
4. **No facts, no times, no clocks.** Exact times belong to service pages and lists, never to a connector (no-clock rule).
5. **RU and EN are register twins**, never literal translations. Write both, or mark the pair unresolved.
6. **Direction — open.** A connector between two sections has no inherent referent. Until the structural fix is chosen (band belongs to the section *below* vs *above* — see the brief), record which section each connector is *meant* to point at, so the copy can survive the decision either way.

## Filling this file

Copy has not been transcribed from Figma yet. Do not reconstruct it from screenshots — connector words are set in outlined display type over photography and misread easily at any reduced size (one word read as "yummy morning" is in fact `YUMMY MORNINGS`). Pull from the file itself, frame by frame.

Once transcribed, review the set per page against rule 2 before touching individual lines — the tone only reads as a set, and the likely edit is cutting the weakest two or three rather than rewriting each.
