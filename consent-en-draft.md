## 2. Purposes and limits

2.1. Considering my enquiry, clarifying necessary information and replying through the contact details I provide, including questions about a booking, order or event and messages to selected team members.

2.2. Retaining the necessary correspondence history so that, at my request, the Operator can provide details of previous enquiries, orders and events, within the period in clause 6.1.

2.3. If my message is an application for a specific vacancy, the data is used to consider that application and contact me about it. The history purpose in clause 2.2 does not apply to applications; clause 6.2 specifies their retention.

2.4. This consent does not authorise advertising mailings, inclusion in a recruitment pool, publication of my message or image, web analytics or connection to Google Maps. These activities require a separate lawful basis and separate consent where necessary.

## 3. Personal data

My name; the email address and/or telephone number I provide; the topic and text of my enquiry and selected team members; information necessary for my question that I supply in subsequent correspondence. For a job application, this also includes the vacancy and experience and qualifications I provide.

To record consent and handle the submission: date and time, form-page language and URL, IP address, consent-text version and a record that consent was given. These details are not used for advertising under this consent.

I should not include passport or payment credentials, health information or other excessive information in an ordinary enquiry. This consent is not intended to cover special categories of data or biometric processing for identification.

## 4. Processing operations

Collection, recording, organisation, accumulation, storage, updating, retrieval, use, transfer (disclosure or access) to deliver, store and consider the enquiry, restriction, deletion and destruction. Processing uses automated systems and non-automated methods. Staff access is limited to work duties; this consent does not authorise public dissemination.

## 5. Hosting and email

The website is hosted on Timeweb. Form messages are sent through Timeweb’s email service and delivered to hello@sweetpepper.bar. Yandex Mail’s collector automatically retrieves messages from that mailbox and stores them in the Operator’s director’s mailbox (p020885@yandex.ru). Copies of enquiries also arrive at the general manager’s work mailbox on Yandex Mail, used for replying to and handling guests’ questions. Yandex Mail therefore receives and stores copies; it is not merely an interface for reading them.

These services receive the enquiry and delivery information, including the consent record, for those purposes. Providers and their roles are described in section 6 of the [Personal Data Processing Policy](https://sweetpepper.bar/en/privacy-policy/). Where processing is entrusted to a provider, the Operator puts the legally required contractual terms in place. This consent does not replace a processing agreement or permit unrestricted disclosure to others.

## 6. Duration and withdrawal

6.1. For ordinary enquiries, consent runs from submission until the relevant purpose is achieved, but for no longer than three years from my last enquiry within the relevant correspondence, or until I withdraw it. After the response, only the history necessary for clause 2.2 is retained. Automated messages from the Operator do not restart this period.

6.2. For a specific job application, consent lasts until its consideration ends or I withdraw consent. Following rejection, data is destroyed within 30 days of the decision. If hired, necessary information is processed on a separate employment-related basis. Data is not added to a recruitment pool.

6.3. I may withdraw consent or ask for correspondence history to stop being retained by emailing hello@sweetpepper.bar or p020885@yandex.ru, or writing to the Operator’s postal address. I should explain my request and give enough information to locate my enquiry. If necessary, the Operator will verify my identity without requesting excessive information.

6.4. When the purpose is achieved, the period expires or consent is withdrawn, the Operator ceases the corresponding processing and destroys the data within 30 days unless another lawful basis applies. If one does, the Operator explains the purpose and further retention period. Requests to cease processing are subject to the specific statutory deadlines. Deletion covers working copies in Timeweb and Yandex Mail and other copies due for deletion under the Policy.

6.5. I can read this separate consent text and the Policy before submitting. Refusing consent does not prevent browsing the website; the form cannot be sent without the consent checkbox. Later changes to the text do not broaden consent I have already given.
