# How it feels — review shortlist

Collected 6 September 2026; expanded 8 September 2026. **20 candidates: 15 Yandex + 5 Google.** All individual ratings were checked: nineteen 5/5 reviews and one 4/5 review. Y11–Y15 were added with English translations on 8 September.

These are short, verbatim Russian excerpts from individual guest reviews, not invented testimonials or translations of the Figma placeholders. Excerpts preserve spelling; surrounding context is noted where it affects selection. Links were obtained through each review's Share control.

English translations added 6 September 2026 for the website. Each appears beneath its Russian original, preserving the guest's meaning, enthusiasm and qualifications. English punctuation is lightly normalized for readability. When published, identify the excerpts as translated from Russian.

## How to use this file

- Cloud words are editorial suggestions, with an English display equivalent. “Literal” means the Russian word occurs in the excerpt (possibly inflected); “interpretive” means the excerpt supports the idea without using that word. These are **not frequency counts or tier assignments**. Repeated words are intentional.
- Names are retained here at the author's explicit request. This overrides `website-brief.md` → reviews pipeline's no-names-storage rule for this research document only; it does not decide whether names appear on the website.
- Yandex dates below use the review card's `datePublished` metadata, which supplies the year omitted by the visible label. Google exposes relative dates in the inspected public interface; those are recorded as observed on 6 September 2026. Exact Google calendar dates remain unverified. Do not convert them into guessed dates.
- This is a selection shortlist, not publication clearance or a representative review dataset. Keep the source link with every selected excerpt. Older Google reviews establish historical experience, not necessarily current operating details.

## Editorial starting point

Start with **Y01, Y02, Y03, Y04, Y09 and G01**. Together they cover a cosy evening, arrival in the city, welcome with a dog, shelter from rain, repeat visits and staff warmth. Use the remaining cards as alternates rather than forcing fifteen different cloud words out of fifteen quotations.

Y10 is the deliberately less comfortable option: it challenges the quiet-only impression of the placeholder cloud. It earns a place in the research, but its criticism should remain visible in the selection decision. No design rule or cloud tier has been changed by this document.

## Access and outstanding verification

- **No Yandex or Google login, owner access, API key or paid export was needed for this shortlist.** Public review pages and their Share controls were sufficient. The Google link's management-style fragment did not open an owner dashboard in this session; the public business panel's reviews button worked.
- **Google exact dates are the remaining metadata gap.** If your signed-in Google Maps or Business Profile view shows exact dates for these five reviews, a copied date or an export you already have would help. Do not share passwords or grant management access just for this document.
- Yandex dates were verified from card metadata. Review text, names and individual star ratings were checked against the public cards; Google ratings were verified from the rendered star icons because the search accessibility text omitted them.
- Review links were captured from the platforms' Share controls, not fabricated from author IDs. The Google short links have not been separately tested in another signed-out browser.
- This small selected pool cannot establish cloud word frequency. Use a separately documented dataset for sizes and tiers, as required by the project brief.
